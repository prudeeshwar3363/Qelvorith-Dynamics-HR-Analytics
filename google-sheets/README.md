# Google Sheets Dashboard

This folder documents the dashboard built in Google Sheets.

## Dashboard Contents

- KPI cards: total employees, employees who left, employees who stayed, and attrition rate
- Department-wise attrition
- Overtime vs. attrition
- Job satisfaction vs. attrition
- Tenure vs. attrition
- Employment type vs. attrition
- Age group vs. attrition
- Commute distance vs. attrition
- Training sessions vs. attrition
- Performance rating vs. attrition
